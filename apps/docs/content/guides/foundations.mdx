<GuideSection id="color" title="Color">
  Every skin carries twelve-step ramps for neutral, primary, and seven hues. Each step has one job, and the same job
  in light and dark: 1–2 backgrounds, 3–5 component fills, 6–8 borders, 9–10 solid fills, 11–12 text. A ramp derives
  from its `--scale-{name}-seed`, so one declaration retints the whole family.

  Components paint roles, never a ramp step: surfaces, text, lines, and the badge families each alias a step. A skin
  re-values a seed or a role and every component follows. Contrast between each pairing is audited on
  [Theme accessibility](/theme-accessibility).

  <ColorFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="typography" title="Typography">
  Two font roles, body and display, default to one sans stack. The type scale is a fixed ladder of rungs; headings
  carry their own line-height, weight, and tracking so a skin can re-voice them without touching components.

<GuideSection id="surfaces" title="Surfaces and layers">
  Canvas is the paper, background and card sit on it, and popovers float above everything. Floating surfaces and the
  modal overlay take their translucency and blur from the surface knobs.

  Stacking follows one ladder of tiers, so layers from different components never compete: `--z-raised` (10),
  `--z-sticky` (20), `--z-nav` (30), `--z-overlay` (70), `--z-modal` (one above the overlay), `--z-popup` (80),
  `--z-toast` (90), and `--z-tooltip` (95). A part that only needs to sit above or below a sibling inside its own
  component uses a local `z-1` or `-z-1` instead of a tier.

  <SurfaceFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="elevation" title="Elevation">
  Every shadow is one recipe scaled by a tier multiplier, then by the global size, opacity, and vertical bias knobs.
  Flatten a skin with `--shadow-size: 0`; deepen one tier by raising its multiplier.

  <ElevationFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="radius" title="Radius and corners">
  One `--radius` knob drives the whole scale: each role is a multiple of it. Nested corners stay concentric through
  the additive tokens — `--radius-popover`, `--radius-composer`, and `--nest-radius` add the inset to the inner
  corner — and `--corner-shape` reshapes every corner where the browser supports it.

  <RadiusFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="sizing" title="Sizing and density">
  `--control-h` is the medium control height, 2.25rem by default. The extra-small, small, and large sizes are 0.78,
  0.89, and 1.11 times that height, snapped to 2px, so one declaration rescales every control and the heights follow
  the user's font size. `--spacing` is the density unit: gaps and padding are multiples of it, including
  `--popover-padding` and `--composer-padding`. Padding knobs set the density of rounded surfaces such as bubbles and
  the composer.

  <SizingFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="motion" title="Motion">
  Three tempos and two curves cover every transition. Reduced-motion settings collapse them to zero globally, so
  components never check the preference themselves.

  <MotionFoundations />
</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="focus" title="Focus">
  The focus indicator is its own role: color, width, style, and offset. It appears on `:focus-visible` only, so a
  pointer press on a button, tab, or checkbox draws nothing, while a text field shows it on click because typing goes
  there next. Each control draws one indicator: fields, the composer, the message being edited, and highlighted menu
  items paint it on their own edge, and everything else gets a ring at `--focus-ring-offset`. A container that owns
  the ring for its parts declares `data-focus-ring="within"`; a field whose surface already shows where focus is, like
  the command palette search, declares `data-focus-ring="none"` and relies on the caret. It must clear 3:1 against
  every surface it lands on.

  <FocusFoundations />
</GuideSection>
